Questions

How do I volunteer or offer my skills?

Open Volunteering and use I Want to Donate Time to see how to connect with us.

How do I ask for help with a project or NGO?

On Volunteering, choose I Need Help and follow the steps for your situation.

How can I contact you?

Email info@freesciencenetwork.org or call 0798839876. We are based in Basel, Switzerland.

What does Free Science Network do?

See what we do on the home page for current focus areas and projects.
